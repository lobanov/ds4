Subject: Medical Research Data Retention Policy Update
To: All Research Investigators
From: Dr. Emily Huang, Chief Medical Officer

Effective immediately, the data retention policy for medical research studies has been updated. All research data must now be retained for a minimum period of 15 years from the study completion date. This policy applies to clinical, behavioral, and genetic research data.

Please review the updated policy and ensure your research data management practices are compliant. Contact the Office of Research Compliance if you have any questions.
