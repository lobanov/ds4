To set up a new account, first navigate to the company's website and locate the "Sign Up" button. Click on it to begin the registration process. You will be prompted to enter your email address, create a secure password, and select a subscription plan that best suits your needs. Once you have filled out all the required fields, click the "Create Account" button to finalize the account setup.

To manage your account settings, log in to your account and click on the "Settings" tab. Under the "Account" section, you will find an option to change your password. Enter your current password, then type in your new desired password and confirm it. Finally, click "Save Changes" to update your password.
