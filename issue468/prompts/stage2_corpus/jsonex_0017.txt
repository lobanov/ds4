The popular social media platform SocialHub has updated its Terms of Service to include stricter penalties for user misconduct. According to the new policy, users who violate the terms may face a range of consequences. Minor offenses, such as sending unsolicited messages or engaging in harassment, can result in a formal warning. Repeated violations or more severe transgressions could lead to account suspension, where a user's access is temporarily revoked. In the most serious cases, SocialHub reserves the right to terminate a user's account entirely if they severely breach the terms, for example by sending spam at a large scale or persistently harassing other members of the community.
