The Bill of Packaging Materials (BOPM) for the manufacturing of high-precision electric motors includes the following components: a lightweight yet durable polycarbonate casing, a shock-absorbing silicone gasket, a corrosion-resistant stainless steel fastener set, and a tamper-evident security seal. The casing dimensions are 120mm x 80mm x 50mm, with a wall thickness of 3mm. The gasket material has a Shore hardness of 60A and a temperature rating of -40°C to 120°C. The fastener set consists of four M4 screws and matching washers. The security seal is made of a tamper-evident polyester film with a unique serial number printed on the surface.
