The monthly equipment maintenance checklist covers a wide range of tasks to ensure the proper functioning and longevity of the manufacturing equipment. This includes inspecting all moving parts for wear and tear, lubricating critical components, cleaning air filters, and verifying the calibration of sensors and gauges. Additionally, the checklist calls for a thorough inspection of the electrical systems, including checking wiring connections, testing safety switches, and confirming the proper operation of motors and control panels. Finally, a visual inspection of the equipment's exterior is performed to identify any signs of damage or corrosion that may require further attention. By completing this comprehensive checklist, the manufacturing team can proactively address any issues and maintain the optimal performance of the equipment.
